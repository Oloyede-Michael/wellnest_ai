1. Product Overview
Product name: WellNest AI
WellNest AI is an AI-powered healthcare companion that helps patients and caregivers understand medical information, manage medications, track treatment plans, and navigate their healthcare journey with confidence.
Rather than functioning as a general AI chatbot, WellNest is designed to become a patient's long-term healthcare companion by organizing medical records into a personalised health journey and providing actionable guidance after every hospital visit.
2. Problem Statement
Every day, patients leave hospitals, clinics, and pharmacies with prescriptions, diagnoses, lab reports, and treatment plans they do not fully understand. Medical information is often filled with technical terminology, delivered within short consultation times, and stored across paper documents or different messaging platforms. This results in patients making avoidable mistakes during treatment.
Challenges patients face
Difficulty understanding diagnoses and medical terms
Confusion about medications and dosage instructions
Missed appointments and follow-up care
Poor medication adherence
Family caregivers lacking access to important health information
Medical records scattered across different platforms
Opportunity
Patients should not leave healthcare facilities confused about their own care.
3. Vision Statement
No patient should leave a healthcare facility confused about their care. WellNest empowers patients with understandable healthcare information and personalised next steps using AI.
4. Product Goals
Primary goal
Help patients understand, organise, and manage their treatment journey through AI-powered healthcare guidance.
Secondary goals
Improve health literacy
Reduce medication errors
Increase treatment adherence
Support family caregivers
Simplify access to personal medical history
5. Target Users
Primary users — Patients
Adults managing acute or chronic health conditions who need help understanding prescriptions, diagnoses, lab results, and treatment plans.
Secondary users — Caregivers
Family members responsible for helping parents, children, spouses, or elderly relatives manage medications and appointments.

7. Solution Overview
WellNest transforms complex medical documents into simple, personalised healthcare guidance.
Core experience — users can:
Upload prescriptions, lab reports, or doctor's notes
Receive AI-generated explanations in simple language
Get personalised medication schedules
Ask follow-up health questions through AI
Track their healthcare journey over time
Share treatment plans with trusted caregivers
8. MVP Features
P0  1. AI Medical Document Upload
Users upload prescriptions, lab reports, or doctor's notes using camera scan, image upload, or PDF upload.
Output
Extracted diagnosis
Medication list
Dosage instructions
Follow-up recommendations
P0  2. AI Diagnosis Translator
AI converts medical terminology into plain, easy-to-understand language.
Medical term
Hypertension
WellNest explanation
Your blood pressure is higher than normal and requires regular monitoring and medication.

P0  3. Medication Planner
Automatically generates a schedule of morning, afternoon, and night medications with dosage instructions and before/after meal reminders. Users can mark medications as taken to track adherence.
P0  4. WellNest AI Assistant
A contextual AI assistant that answers questions using the patient's uploaded medical information.
Example questions
What does my diagnosis mean?
Can I take this medicine after eating?
What foods should I avoid?
What happens if I miss a dose?
P1  5. Family Care Mode
Patients invite trusted caregivers, who can view medication schedules, receive reminders, track appointments, and access emergency health information with permission.
P1  6. Health Journey Timeline
A chronological timeline showing diagnoses, uploaded reports, medication updates, appointments, and treatment progress.
P1  7. Emergency Health Wallet
Stores blood group, allergies, existing medical conditions, emergency contacts, and current medications for use in an emergency.
9. End-to-End Product Flow
Step 1 — Onboarding
Launch WellNest
Sign up or log in
Select Patient or Caregiver
Complete profile information
Enter Home Dashboard
Step 2 — Upload medical document
Tap “Upload Document”
Scan or upload prescription, report, or doctor's note
Submit document for AI analysis
Step 3 — AI analysis
WellNest AI reads the document using OCR, identifies diagnoses and medications, extracts dosage and treatment instructions, and generates a simplified health summary.
Step 4 — Health summary
The patient receives a diagnosis explanation, plain-language summary, recommended actions, risk indicators, and follow-up suggestions.
Step 5 — Medication plan
AI creates a personalised medication schedule with reminders and adherence tracking.
Step 6 — Ask WellNest AI
Users ask questions about their diagnosis or medications and receive contextual answers based on uploaded records.
Step 7 — Family care
Users share treatment plans with caregivers, who receive reminders and updates.
Step 8 — Health journey dashboard
WellNest continuously updates the patient's health timeline and progress.

18. Expected Impact
WellNest aims to improve healthcare accessibility by helping patients better understand their medical information, adhere to treatment plans, and involve caregivers throughout their healthcare journey.
By combining AI with a patient-centred experience, WellNest reduces confusion after hospital visits and empowers users to make informed health decisions.

19. Frontend Implementation Status
A working frontend for the WellNest AI MVP has been built and is ready for the team to review, extend, and connect to the AI/OCR backend described in this document.
Stack
Vite + React 19
Tailwind CSS (custom design system — see tokens below)
React Router for client-side navigation
lucide-react icon set
Screens implemented
Dashboard — health status hero, weekly adherence, quick actions, recent activity, latest vitals
Documents — scan / upload / browse, drag-and-drop, recent documents list
Medications — weekly adherence chart, time-of-day schedule, mark-as-taken, reminder toggles
Ask WellNest AI — chat interface with quick-prompt chips and contextual replies
Health Timeline — chronological record of diagnoses, documents, medication changes, appointments
Family Care — caregiver list, access levels, invite flow
Emergency Health Wallet — blood group, allergies, conditions, medications, emergency contacts
Settings — account information and notification preferences
Design system
Indigo #2C49C0 (primary / brand, from the WellNest logo)
Meadow green #2E9B4F (adherence and positive states)
Coral #D5473D (emergency and alerts)
Type: Sora for headings, IBM Plex Sans for body and data
What's not built yet
The frontend currently runs on mock data (src/data/mockData.js) so the team can review the full experience without a backend. The OCR pipeline, medical NLP engine, AI assistant, authentication, and persistence described in sections 12 and 14 still need to be built and wired in — the components are already shaped to accept that data once it exists.
